SevenKeys

Seven lanes. A S D · Space · J K L. Hit the notes as they cross the hit line. Holds must stay pressed until the tail arrives.

Pick a local MP3 and the analyser estimates the tempo, measures how well the beat grid matches the music, finds the onsets and builds a seven-lane chart you can play straight away. After importing, Diagnostics shows the numbers and an onset / beat / note timeline you can play back with clicks over the music. The demo chart stays available for comparison.

Rotate your device

Seven Keys plays in landscape: seven lane columns need the full width of the screen, and the touch pads belong under your thumbs. Turn the phone sideways to keep playing.

Fullscreen also enables the landscape orientation lock on Android Chrome. Firefox does not support locking, so it will simply stay rotated by hand.
